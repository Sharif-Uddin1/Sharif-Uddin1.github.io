Research Interests

Antenna Design

Design, simulation, fabrication, and measurement of advanced antenna structures.

Circular Polarization

Wideband circularly polarized antennas and polarization control techniques.

MIMO Antennas

Multi-port antenna systems, isolation enhancement, and MIMO performance optimization.

Full-Duplex Communication

Antenna technologies for simultaneous transmit and receive wireless communication.

RF & Microwave

RF systems, microwave engineering, measurement, and electromagnetic analysis.

mmWave & THz

Antenna and electromagnetic technologies for millimeter-wave and terahertz applications.

Current Research Projects

My current research focuses on the design, simulation, fabrication, and experimental characterization of advanced RF and antenna systems for next-generation wireless communications.

Differentially Fed Wideband Circularly Polarized Antenna for Full-Duplex Communication

Current Research

Developing a wideband circularly polarized antenna architecture for full-duplex wireless systems, with emphasis on differential feeding, polarization purity, and high isolation between transmitting and receiving channels.

Keywords: Circular Polarization · Full Duplex · Differential Feeding · Antenna Isolation
View Project ↗

Wideband Circularly Polarized Antenna for Relay Applications

Current Research

Developing a wideband circularly polarized antenna for wireless relay applications, with emphasis on polarization diversity, wide impedance bandwidth, and reliable communication between transmitting and receiving nodes.

Keywords: Circular Polarization · Relay Communications · Wideband Antennas · RF/Microwave · Wireless Communications

View Project ↗

Compact Circularly Polarized MIMO Antenna for High-Data-Rate Communications

Published Research

Investigating compact circularly polarized MIMO antenna architectures for high-data-rate wireless communications, with emphasis on isolation enhancement and experimentally validated antenna performance.

Keywords: MIMO · Circular Polarization · Isolation Enhancement · High Data Rate
View Publication ↗

RF Integrated Circuit, Antenna-on-chip, mmWave/THz Antenna Systems

Research Direction

Exploring antenna architectures and electromagnetic structures for high-frequency wireless communication systems, with interest in integrated circuits, mmWave and terahertz technologies, high-gain structures, and potential RF front-end integration.

Keywords: RF · mmWave · THz · High-Gain Antennas · RF Front-End
